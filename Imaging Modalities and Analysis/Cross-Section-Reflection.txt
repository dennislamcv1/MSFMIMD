Cross-Section Reflection - Contoso Imaging Dataset Readiness Assessment

Section 2 recorded that CT slice thickness differs by site: Site A reconstructs at 1.25 mm, while Sites B and C use 2.5 mm. That finding changed how I read the 2.5 mm reconstructions in CT-04 and CT-06 in Section 4: they are a systematic site-level pattern, not two one-off deviations. It also showed me that the Proceed status for CT Studies 1, 3 and 5 in Section 1 covered image quality only, because Section 1 records neither site nor reconstruction thickness. This matters for the model configuration team because a site-linked reconstruction difference changes spatial resolution, texture and noise according to where a scan was acquired, so two of the eight CT protocol studies differ from the reference and site can become a hidden variable in model inputs. The team therefore needs a reconstruction-thickness and site tag on every CT study at handoff, including the Section 1 samples, so the 2.5 mm inputs are stratified or harmonized on purpose rather than silently mixed with 1.25 mm reference data. It also turns two study-level fixes into one request to Sites B and C for 1.25 mm reconstructions where they are available.
