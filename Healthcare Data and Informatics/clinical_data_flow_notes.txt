CLINICAL DATA FLOW DIAGNOSTIC DRAFT (REGIONAL HOSPITAL AI REVIEW)

Course framework: Generation -> Transmission -> Storage -> Retrieval -> Linkage

Stage 1: Generation
- Imaging request/order: RIS/HIS creates an order_id, accession number, patient/encounter context, and requested examination.
- Imaging study: the modality creates the DICOM study; the Study Instance UID uniquely identifies the study within the imaging workflow.
- Diagnostic report: the reporting system creates a report_id and final report text after image review.
- Scenario evidence: RAD-2026-0893 has no report_encounter_id in the downstream report metadata; RAD-2026-0894 has no dicom_study_uid in the supplied integrated extract.

Stage 2: Transmission
- DICOM imaging objects are transmitted from the modality to the imaging archive/PACS.
- Order, report, and encounter metadata are exchanged or exported to downstream clinical/data systems using the interfaces represented in this scenario.
- Identifiers must remain distinct and traceable across handoffs; accession number and Study Instance UID are not interchangeable identifiers.

Stage 3: Storage
- DICOM studies are stored in the imaging archive/PACS.
- Final diagnostic reports and patient/encounter metadata are stored in the clinical record systems represented in the scenario.
- Prepared NLP output is retained as a separate derived text artifact with report_id provenance.

Stage 4: Retrieval
- The project pipeline retrieves the prepared DICOM study references/metadata, NLP JSON, and clinical metadata for creation of the bounded evaluation dataset.
- Records with missing or inconsistent identifiers are retained and flagged for review rather than silently dropped or assigned fabricated values.

Stage 5: Linkage
- Report-to-study linkage is evaluated using the supplied request/order, accession, patient/encounter, and study-reference evidence.
- The DICOM Study Instance UID identifies the imaging study; accession number supports imaging workflow correlation but is a distinct identifier.
- Scenario evidence: RAD-2026-0895 has a report_accession_number that does not match the supplied study_accession_number. RAD-2026-0896 has inconsistent report versus study modality metadata.

Review focus
- Determine whether patient/encounter context is preserved.
- Determine whether report-to-study linkage is supported by consistent evidence.
- Record missing identifiers and inconsistent metadata as explicit gaps.
- Do not infer or manufacture a missing identifier from date/time proximity alone.
